# How to Use the Referral Program

## Overview
Earn free access to PipeKeeper, WhiskeyKeeper, CigarKeeper, and WineKeeper by inviting friends. Get rewarded when they subscribe.

## Getting Started

### 1. Access Your Referral Dashboard
- Log in to your account
- Navigate to **ReferralDashboard** in the main menu
- Your unique referral code and link appear at the top

### 2. Share Your Referral Link
Three ways to share:

**Copy Link**
- Click the **Copy Link** button
- Paste it in messages, emails, or social media
- The link automatically tracks clicks and signups

**Email Invites**
- Click **Send Email Invite**
- Enter friend's email address
- Add an optional personal message
- Hit send (system delivers the invite)

**Native Share**
- Click the **Share** button (mobile)
- Opens your device's native share sheet
- Share via any app on your phone

### 3. Track Your Progress
On the dashboard, you'll see:
- **Invites Sent**: How many emails you've sent
- **Links Copied**: How many times you've copied your link
- **Recipient Clicks**: Unique clicks on your link
- **Qualified Referrals**: Friends who subscribed (and passed fraud checks)
- **Earned Free Months/Years**: Total rewards accumulated

### 4. Monitor Your Rewards
**Pending Rewards** section shows:
- Reward type (free month or free year)
- Billing provider (Stripe, iOS, or earned access)
- Current status (pending, ready to apply, applied, redeemed)
- Fulfillment progress

### 5. Claim Your Free Access (iOS Users)

If you earned free access without an active subscription:

1. The system notifies you when a reward is ready
2. Click **Redeem** on the reward card
3. Select which module to unlock (PipeKeeper, WhiskeyKeeper, CigarKeeper, or WineKeeper)
4. Confirm selection
5. Module access is activated immediately for the earned duration (1 month or 1 year)

**Billing Provider Notes:**
- **Stripe Subscribers**: Rewards apply as account credits automatically
- **iOS Subscribers**: Redeem in-app via StoreKit (one click, no additional payment)
- **Free Users**: Select module at redemption time; enjoy free access for the earned period

### 6. Extend Existing Access
If you have an active subscription in a module:
- A referral reward for the same module **extends** your period
- No time lost between periods
- Access period shifts later, starting fresh

### 7. Multiple Modules
- Earn rewards for different modules independently
- Each module has its own access window
- You can have free access to multiple modules simultaneously

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario A: You're a Stripe Subscriber
1. Invite friends → they subscribe
2. When qualified, your reward auto-applies as a billing credit
3. Your next billing cycle skips or reduces (credit covers it)
4. Check your Stripe portal for billing details

### Scenario B: You're an iOS Subscriber
1. Invite friends → they subscribe
2. Reward shows as "ready to apply" in your dashboard
3. Click **Redeem** and confirm the module selection
4. Access activates immediately
5. Your subscription renews normally at its scheduled date

### Scenario C: You're a Free User
1. Invite friends → they subscribe
2. Reward appears in your dashboard
3. Click **Redeem** and select a module
4. Start using that module free for 1 month (or 1 year)
5. When access expires, module locks (you can upgrade anytime)

### Scenario D: Multiple Referrals
1. You have 2 free months earned
2. Redeem first month for PipeKeeper → access starts
3. Redeem second month for WhiskeyKeeper → separate access window
4. Both active simultaneously; they expire independently

## Tips for Success

**Maximize Referrals:**
- Share with friends who actively collect (pipes, cigars, whiskey, wine)
- Include a personal message ("Check out PipeKeeper—I've catalogued 100+ pipes!")
- Share on social media where collectors gather

**Track Performance:**
- Review your referral stats weekly
- Note which sharing method works best (email vs link copy)
- Adjust your outreach based on click-through rates

**Understand Qualification:**
- Friend must sign up and complete trial or subscription
- They must pass our fraud checks (automatic)
- No manual review needed for legitimate referrals
- Timeframe: typically within 24 hours of their subscription
