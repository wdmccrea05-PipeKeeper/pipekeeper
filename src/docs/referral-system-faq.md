# Referral Program FAQ

## Earning Rewards

**Q: How much free access do I get per referral?**
A: One qualified referral = one free month. Earn additional months by referring more friends. No cap on total rewards.

**Q: What counts as a "qualified referral"?**
A: A friend who:
1. Signs up using your referral link or code
2. Starts a paid subscription (or completes trial → converts to paid)
3. Passes automated fraud checks (legitimate signup, no red flags)

**Q: Can I earn a free year instead of monthly?**
A: The system awards free months. However, if you prefer annual access, you can:
- Accumulate 12 free months and redeem them together, OR
- Contact support to discuss consolidation options

**Q: Do free trials count as subscriptions?**
A: Trial users must convert to a paid subscription to qualify your referral. A trial alone doesn't trigger a reward.

**Q: How long does it take for a referral to be counted?**
A: Typically 24–48 hours after your friend completes their subscription. Automated fraud checks run in real-time; legitimate referrals process quickly.

**Q: What if my friend uses my link but doesn't complete signup?**
A: They appear in your "Recipient Clicks" metric, but no reward is earned. They must complete signup and subscribe.

**Q: Can I see who I referred?**
A: You see aggregated stats (total invites, clicks, qualified referrals). For privacy, individual names aren't disclosed unless the friend opts in.

## Rewards & Redemption

**Q: Can I transfer my earned free access to someone else?**
A: No. Earned access is tied to your account and can't be gifted or transferred.

**Q: What if I already have a paid subscription and earn a reward for the same module?**
A: Your subscription period extends. No time is lost. If your WhiskeyKeeper sub ends April 30, a free month reward shifts that to May 30.

**Q: Can I stack multiple rewards?**
A: Yes. If you earn 3 free months:
- Use 1 month for PipeKeeper (access ends 30 days from today)
- Use 1 month for WhiskeyKeeper (separate 30-day window)
- Use 1 month for CigarKeeper (separate 30-day window)
All three run independently.

**Q: What happens if I don't redeem a reward?**
A: Free access rewards don't expire. You can redeem them anytime. For Stripe users, rewards apply automatically as credits (no action needed).

**Q: Can I earn free access to multiple modules at once?**
A: Yes. Each referral = one reward. Redeem each for a different module and run simultaneous access.

**Q: Does my subscription continue after free access expires?**
A: It depends:
- **If you had a paid sub before**: It resumes at your regular renewal date
- **If you were on free access**: Module locks; you need to upgrade or earn another reward

## Billing & Providers

**Q: How do Stripe rewards work?**
A: Rewards auto-apply as account credits. Your next billing cycle skips or reduces cost by the credit amount. Check your Stripe portal or account settings for billing details.

**Q: How do iOS (in-app) rewards work?**
A: Click **Redeem** in your dashboard, select a module, and confirm. Access activates within seconds. StoreKit handles the transaction on Apple's backend (no extra charge).

**Q: Do I pay anything to redeem a reward?**
A: No. Referral rewards are always free—zero cost to redeem or use.

**Q: What if I have multiple subscriptions (e.g., Stripe + iOS)?**
A: Rewards apply separately per provider. A Stripe reward applies as a credit to your Stripe billing. An iOS reward activates in-app. Each is independent.

**Q: Does a free access reward convert to a paid subscription?**
A: No. Free access expires and your account reverts to free tier (unless you have another active subscription). To continue after free access, you must upgrade.

## Fraud & Verification

**Q: Why was my referral rejected/flagged?**
A: Our system checks for:
- Self-referrals (you can't refer yourself)
- Suspicious patterns (rapid signups, shared IP, unusual device behavior)
- Duplicate accounts
If flagged, support reviews and contacts you if needed.

**Q: How can I appeal a rejected referral?**
A: Contact support with details (referrer email, referred friend's email, signup date). We'll review and reinstate if the referral is legitimate.

**Q: Is there a limit to how many people I can refer?**
A: No. Refer as many friends as you want.

**Q: Can I earn rewards if I'm in the same household as a friend?**
A: Yes, but accounts must be distinct and signups must appear legitimate (not rapid, coordinated, or bot-like). Household members can refer each other without issue.

## Sharing & Tracking

**Q: Can I customize my referral link?**
A: Your link is auto-generated and tied to your account. It's unique and can't be customized, but it's simple and easy to share.

**Q: What data does my referral link track?**
A: Clicks and signups only. No personal browsing data or collection info is tracked.

**Q: Can I see which invites converted to subscriptions?**
A: You see totals (qualified referrals count), but not individual names for privacy. However, you can estimate: compare "Invites Sent" to "Qualified Referrals."

**Q: Can I revoke a referral link?**
A: Your referral link is permanent and can't be disabled. If you'd prefer to stop sharing, simply don't distribute it further.

**Q: Do referral links expire?**
A: No. Your link never expires and can be reused indefinitely.

## Module Selection & Access

**Q: Can I switch which module my free access is for?**
A: Once you redeem a reward for a module, that access window is locked to that module. However, you can:
- Redeem future rewards for different modules
- Let the access expire and redeem a new reward for another module

**Q: What if I redeem free access but don't use the module?**
A: The access window still counts down. If unused after 30 days, it expires. No refund, but you can earn additional rewards to try again.

**Q: Can I upgrade from free access to a paid subscription mid-period?**
A: Yes. Your free access period ends, and your paid subscription begins immediately. Your free period is not pro-rated or refunded.

## General

**Q: Is there a minimum referral bonus threshold?**
A: No. You earn rewards for every qualified referral—no threshold to reach.

**Q: Can I use a referral reward and a subscription at the same time?**
A: If you have earned free access to WhiskeyKeeper and a paid subscription to PipeKeeper, both are active simultaneously (different modules).

**Q: What if my friend uses my link but has a payment issue?**
A: If they don't complete payment (subscription fails), your referral isn't qualified. Once they successfully subscribe, your reward activates.

**Q: Do I get notified when someone uses my referral link?**
A: You'll see clicks aggregate in your "Recipient Clicks" stat. When they qualify, you'll be notified that a reward was earned.

**Q: Can I refer someone who's already an account holder?**
A: If they're an existing user (already have an account), referral rewards don't apply. Both parties must be authenticated correctly for the system to count it.
